When reporting a problem, please include your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened — that is usually enough to reproduce it.

How do I play?

Tap an orbital slot to build a defence, tap a built defence to upgrade or sell it. Enemies arrive in waves along the marked approach; anything that reaches your world costs you integrity. The in-game HELP screen covers the full rules, and CODEX holds a dossier on every enemy you have encountered.

Where did my progress go?

Progress is stored on the device. It survives updates, but deleting the app deletes it. If you have iCloud enabled for Cosmic Ascent, your record follows your Apple Account to your other devices; the most recently played device wins if two of them disagree.

I lost a run when I got a phone call

The game pauses itself when it goes to the background, and the campaign keeps a checkpoint at the start of each wave — so an interruption costs you at most the wave in progress, never the run. Choose CONTINUE from the main menu to pick it back up.

Can I turn off the music, sound or vibration?

Yes — all three are separate switches on the pause screen. Haptics also follow the system's Reduce Motion setting automatically.

The game feels too hard / too easy

Difficulty is selectable before a run and changes enemy toughness and the economy, not just the numbers on screen. Ascension points earned in one run permanently strengthen the next, so an early loss is not wasted.

Accessibility

The game honours Reduce Motion (screen shake, heavy particle effects and haptics are suppressed) and runs entirely on tap input with no timed gestures.
