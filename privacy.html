Privacy Policy

Cosmic Ascent — last updated 9 August 2026

Cosmic Ascent does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no user accounts, and no third-party SDK of any kind. The developer receives nothing about you or how you play.

What the game stores

Your progress — campaign saves, ascension points, records, unlocked codex entries and your sound, music and haptics settings — is written to your device's own local storage. Deleting the app removes all of it.

iCloud sync

If iCloud is enabled for Cosmic Ascent in your device settings, that same progress record is mirrored through your own iCloud account so it follows you between your devices. It is stored under your Apple Account, governed by Apple's privacy policy, and the developer has no access to it and no way to read it. Turning iCloud off for the app keeps everything local.

The App Store rating prompt

After a campaign victory the game may ask Apple to show the standard "rate this app" panel. The panel is presented by the operating system, not by the game; anything you write in it goes to the App Store, and the game is not told whether you rated it or what you said.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children specifically. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If a future version ever changes what the app stores or sends, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
